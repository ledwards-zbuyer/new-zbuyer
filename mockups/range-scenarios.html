Estimate ranges — scenario gallery

The three estimate types as ranges + point values — each dot is pinned to the curve at its type's single number, with the range running horizontally through it. The handle is live: drag it and watch the headline tick — the value updates while held, the estimate you're inside bolds, its dot pops as you glide over the point, and release snaps to the nearest point. The labeled bar under each chart is the complete range (the union of all values), where the untouched handle parks at center. All cards are live renders of assets/value-slider.js. Colors are the shared map: Cash navy, AI sky, AVM primary. See it arriving live on the report (?ranges=1) · developer guide · slider demo page · five alternate display concepts.

Agreement — all three types land close together

Heavily overlapping ranges, points within ~$7K of each other. The stagger logic keeps near-tied terraces legible.

Disparate — the three disagree

Little to no overlap; the domain stretches to hold everything and each terrace sits clearly apart.

Wide ranges — low confidence

$45–60K spans. Long overlapping lines; the points do the talking.

Narrow ranges — high confidence

$6–10K spans. Short dashes with their dots — reads almost like three point estimates.

AI = AVM — the exact tie

Identical ranges: each line still sits at its own point's curve height, so the pair reads as a tight stack; the labels find opposite ends. Only the points differ.

Cash discount — the classic shape

Cash offer clearly below the AI/AVM pair — the expected report shape (this is what ?ranges=1 on the report demos).

One uncertain — AVM wide, the others tight

Mixed confidence: a sprawling AVM band against tight Cash and AI ranges.

Points at the edges

Point values sitting exactly on a range's low or high end (and one passed OUTSIDE its range, which clamps in) — the dots hold the line ends cleanly.

Single values only — no ranges supplied

Each type passed just its number ({ id, value, label }): the widget synthesizes a ±10% range around it, rounded reasonably — snap to a dot and the synthesized range shows under the value.
